# Ysello 2026-09-08.6 — Professional Category Hierarchy

## Category structure
- Simplified social listings to a three-level flow: `Social Accounts → Platform → Type`.
- Removed the redundant extra `Accounts` layer.
- Social account types now use concise labels: `Fresh`, `Aged`, `Followers`, `Posts`.
- Canonical platform slugs are preserved for stable storefront routes and Dark Shopping classification.
- Old bridge categories such as `instagram-creator-tools` are retired to prevent duplicate platform choices.

## Storefront navigation
- Global navigation shows only departments and immediate platforms/subcategories.
- Leaf listing types stay inside their platform/category pages instead of crowding the header, homepage, and catalog sidebar.
- Product counts roll up from leaf types to their platform and department, so populated platforms remain visible even when products are stored in subcategories.
- Mobile subcategories use a compact two-column grid instead of long horizontal cards.

## Seller and admin listing flow
- Social listing flow is now `Category → Platform → Type`.
- Removed redundant Product/Account steps and repeated labels.
- Platform/type summaries are concise and synchronized from the selected path.
- Account condition logic now uses stable category slugs rather than visible label text.

## Additional taxonomy polish
- Shortened broad marketplace labels such as `Games → Platforms / Genres / Releases / Gift cards / Subscriptions / Top-ups / Currency`.
- Shortened Software, Gift Card, Social, and Deals labels where the previous wording was unnecessarily long.

## Validation
- Changed TypeScript/TSX files were parsed successfully with TypeScript 5.8.3.
- Navigation rollup sanity test passed: leaf product counts correctly surface on platform and root categories while leaf types remain out of global navigation.
