# Ysello 2026-09-08.1

Baseline: 2026-09-07.8 (the stable build requested by the owner).

## Visitor intelligence
- Human visitor alerts wait for ~35 seconds on-site and at least 30 visible seconds.
- Tracks pages viewed and basic interaction count before classifying an engaged visitor.
- Server-side crawler alerts remain separate and use crawler user-agent detection.
- Static assets (SVG, images, CSS, JS, fonts, maps, archives, PDFs) are excluded from visitor alerts.
- Country/city first use proxy/Vercel headers, then a short-timeout GeoIP fallback when headers are absent.
- Alerts include IP, country/city, browser, OS, device, language, timezone, screen, referrer, search/referral source, time on site, pages viewed, interactions, and canonical ysello.com page URL.

## Buyer purchase flow
- Buy/order actions retain the selected product slug in cart and checkout URLs.
- Added refresh-safe private SPA routes for /cart/:productSlug and /checkout/:productSlug.
- Wallet purchase no longer clears the cart before navigation, preventing the empty-cart redirect race.
- Completed orders open a real confirmation/delivery view instead of “Your cart is quiet.”
- Confirmation loads the created order and exposes direct downloads (ZIP/files/account delivery), invoice, seller chat and dispute actions on the same page.
- Checkout uses premium product artwork instead of plain product icons.
- Product titles are no longer visually truncated on mobile purchase flow.
- Order delivery workspace now shows product imagery in order/product records.
- Existing buyer orders/download library image treatment is normalized to white, contained premium artwork cards.

## Seller storefront
- Sellers can now edit their public store name from Storefront settings.
- Store rename regenerates a safe unique public store slug.
- Existing logo and banner replacement controls remain available in the same form.
