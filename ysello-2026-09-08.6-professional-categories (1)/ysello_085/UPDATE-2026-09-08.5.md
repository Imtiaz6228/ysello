# Ysello 2026-09-08.5

## Dark Shopping full-catalog integration

- Added one-click Admin action to import and publish the complete in-stock, automatic-delivery Dark Shopping catalog.
- Added automatic supplier catalog discovery shortly after Railway startup and every six hours when `DARK_SHOPPING_API_KEY` is configured.
- Existing Dark Shopping listing sync continues to refresh imported stock/pricing separately.
- Social products are classified from supplier title, description, category, group and attributes into New Accounts, Old/Aged Accounts, Accounts with Followers, or Accounts with Posts.
- Non-social products use meaningful supplier groups as Ysello child categories when available.
- Existing Dark imports are reorganized by the repair action into the same taxonomy.
- Captures supplier remote category/group IDs, attributes, quantity, minimum order, remote media, guarantee, replacement terms, quality/invalid percentages, views, purchase counter and supplier rating internally.
- Public product pages/cards do not expose supplier sales counters or ratings.

## Public product presentation

- Removed rating/review totals and total-sales counters from public product cards.
- Removed rating/sales summary and public review-rating section from the product detail page.
- Removed AggregateRating structured data so search results do not surface product ratings from Ysello.
